## About these notes

These are notes from a two-day special lecture in abstract algebra, held on 25 and 26 November 2026. The first lecture follows Chapter I of Serre's *A Course in Arithmetic*, on finite fields, whose goal is Gauss's quadratic reciprocity law. The second lecture, on the second day, is about Pythagorean triples.

- *Day 1 (25 November):* finite fields and the Frobenius map, existence and uniqueness of $\mathbb F_q$, the cyclicity of $\mathbb F_q^*$, squares in $\mathbb F_q$, the Legendre symbol, and the Gauss sum up to its first lemma, $y^2 = (-1)^{\varepsilon(\ell)}\ell$.
- *Day 2 (26 November):* the second lemma on the Gauss sum and the proof of quadratic reciprocity, computing Legendre symbols, and Eisenstein's proof (appendix); then a second lecture on Pythagorean triples: their classification, rational points on the unit circle, consecutive sides, reducible quadratic pairs, and rational parametrizations of conics.

Only what was covered in the lecture is written out. Parts that were skipped keep their headings, marked as not covered, so that the numbering still matches Serre.

## How these notes are organised

Sections follow Serre, Chapter I: §1 finite fields, §2 equations over a finite field, §3 the quadratic reciprocity law. Theorems and lemmas keep the numbers they have on the lecture slides (Theorem 1–6); lemmas are numbered within their section, so "Lemma 1" in §1.2 and "Lemma 1" in §3.3 are different statements. The lecture on Pythagorean triples has its own sections 1–5 and slide numbers (Lemma 1.1, Theorem 1.2, …).
